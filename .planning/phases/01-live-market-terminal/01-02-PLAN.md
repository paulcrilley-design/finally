## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** open the app and watch prices stream live, **so that** I can follow the market at a glance.

<objective>
Build the Walking Skeleton, one thin path only: one FastAPI app creates and seeds the database, runs the existing market simulator on the seeded tickers, serves the price stream and a static Next.js page, and that page shows those ten tickers with live prices.

Output: `backend/db/database.py`, the app factory, a hand-written `frontend/` and the end-to-end test.

Later plans widen this path: the stored watchlist and the identity function (01-11), data guarantees and the run command (01-03), frontend tests and lint (01-04), openUI tokens and the module split (01-06). Add none of their files here.
</objective>
